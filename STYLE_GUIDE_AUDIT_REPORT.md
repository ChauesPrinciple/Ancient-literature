## ⚠️ MINOR ISSUES IDENTIFIED

### Issue 1: Self-Announcement Language (Professor's Voice)
**Location:** Multiple Professor's Analysis files
**Pattern Found:** "I want to introduce another idea"

**Example:**
```
File: module-9-song-of-roland/05-song-of-roland-discussion.html
Line 69: "I want to introduce another idea. Later in the semester, we will..."
```

**Style Guide Rule Violated:**
- Forbidden: Self-announcements ("I want to", "I think", "I believe")

**Recommended Fix:**
```
CURRENT: "I want to introduce another idea."
REVISED: "Another important concept to consider is..."
OR: "Let's examine another perspective:"
```

**Severity:** LOW - This is professor's teaching voice, not student writing. However, modeling the preferred academic voice would strengthen the materials.
